A verifiable record

Each publication has a signed identity and fixed contents. Anyone can download, verify, and mirror a complete copy.

Corrections are new publications. Citations connect results and keep the earlier record available.

Publishing terms

Text, figures, and data: CC BY 4.0. Code: MIT.

By signing, the publisher agrees to these terms and confirms authority to distribute all included material under them. Retain required attribution and copyright notices. Reference material that cannot meet these terms externally.

Hosting rules are separate. Hosted material must contribute research with relevant supporting files. Negative results are welcome.
